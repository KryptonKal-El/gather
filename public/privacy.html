Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Introduction

Gather Lists ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the iPhone and iPad app and the website at gatherlists.com.

Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address: used to create your account, sign you in, and send sign-in codes and sharing invitations. If you use Sign in with Apple you can choose Apple's private relay address.
  • Name: the display name shown to people you share with. You can change it in Settings.
  • Passkeys: if you add a passkey we store its public key and a label such as "Apple Passwords (iPhone)". The private key never leaves your device or password manager.

Your content

  • Lists and the items in them, including notes, quantities, prices, categories and stores
  • Recipes, collections, tags and cooking history
  • Meal plans
  • Photos you add to items or recipes

Sharing

  • When you invite someone, we store their email address so the invitation reaches them and the shared content appears in their account.

Subscription information

  • If you subscribe to Gather Lists Pro, we store which plan you have, when it renews or ends, and Apple's reference number for the purchase, so Pro works on all your devices and on the web.
  • Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card or billing details.

Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, we store the push token Apple issues for your device so we can deliver them.

Feature usage counts

  • We record when your account uses an image lookup, an online recipe search or a recipe import, so the monthly limits can be applied. We record that the feature was used, not what you searched for.

Searches and imports

  • Product, image and recipe searches are sent to our search providers to fetch results. They are sent without your name, email or account details, and we do not keep a record of them.
  • When you import a recipe from a web address, our server fetches that page to read the recipe.

What we do not collect

  • Location, contacts, health data or financial details
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or crash reports from third-party tracking tools

On-Device Intelligence

Some features, such as reading pasted recipe text, suggesting recipe tags and understanding a note about your week, use the language model built into your Apple device. That processing happens on your device. These features are only available on devices that support it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To run your account: signing you in and keeping you signed in
  • To provide the app: storing, syncing and sharing your lists, recipes and meal plans
  • To make suggestions: your own past items and recipes are used to suggest items and meals to you
  • To deliver notifications and invitations you or the people you share with have asked for
  • To provide Gather Lists Pro and apply the limits of the free plan

We do not use your information for advertising.

Data Storage

Your data is stored with Supabase on Amazon Web Services infrastructure. It is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Track you across other apps or websites

We share data only in these cases:

  • With people you share with: they can see the shared list, recipe collection or meal plan, and your display name and email address
  • With apps you connect: if you connect an assistant or other app to your account in Settings, it can read and change your content within the access you approve, until you disconnect it
  • With the service providers listed below, only as needed to run the app

Service Providers

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage (privacy policy)
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and subscription purchases (privacy policy)
  • Resend: delivery of sign-in and invitation emails
  • Vercel: hosting for the website
  • Spoonacular, Open Food Facts, Walmart and SerpAPI: product, image and recipe search results (search text only)

Your Choices and Rights

  • Access: your lists, recipes, meal plans and account details are visible in the app
  • Correct: you can edit your content and display name at any time
  • Delete: you can delete any list, recipe or meal plan in the app. To delete your account and all its data, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This also removes lists, collections and meal plans you shared with others
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel Gather Lists Pro in your Apple account settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings

Children's Privacy

Gather Lists is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Data Retention

  • Account data and content are kept while your account is active
  • Content you delete is removed from our servers
  • When an account is deleted, its content, photos and personal details are removed. A record of any subscription is kept without your account details so Apple's billing updates can still be processed

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about material changes.

Contact Us

  • Email: support@gatherlists.com
  • Website: gatherlists.com

This privacy policy is effective as of November 20, 2026.
